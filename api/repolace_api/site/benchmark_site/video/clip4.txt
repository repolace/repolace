A power curve for comparing two configurations on these 18 bugs. The horizontal axis is the true gap in pass rate in points; the vertical axis is the chance of detecting it. The bootstrap standard error of a pass rate here is about 9.9 points, so a difference between two runs has a standard error near 14 points. The curve reaches 80 percent power at a gap of about 39 points. The observed gaps from the baseline are about 7 points for the early-edit variant, 9 points for the role split and 22 points for the mixed-model run. All three sit well to the left, where the chance of detecting a real difference is low. That is why no variant can be called better or worse on these data.
