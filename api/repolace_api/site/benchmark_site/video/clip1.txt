Fifty-four dots, one for each run: filled when the fix passed the hidden tests, hollow when it did not. They regroup into 18 boxes of three, one box per bug. Because the three runs of one bug are not independent, the unit that can be resampled is the bug, not the run. The animation draws 18 bugs with replacement, adds up their passes, and marks the resulting pass rate on an axis. Repeating this many times builds a histogram of possible pass rates. The middle 95 percent of that histogram runs from 44 percent to 83 percent, around the observed 35 of 54 passes.
