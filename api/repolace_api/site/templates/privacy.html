Privacy notice

Waitlist stage. Last updated 2 November 2026.

What this site collects

If you join the waitlist we store your email address and the time you joined. That is all the form collects. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies, and this site sets no cookies. Your theme choice (light, dark or system) is kept in your browser only.

Why, and for how long

We use the address to tell you when early access opens, and for nothing else. We keep it until you ask us to delete it or until the waitlist closes, whichever is first. We do not sell or share it.

Who handles it

The site runs behind Cloudflare, which sees requests to it (including your IP address) in order to deliver and protect the site. Where the human check is switched on, Cloudflare Turnstile also processes a token about your browser. The address itself is stored in our own database.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your address, write to the contact address on the rounakkumarsingh.com site and we will do it.

This notice describes the waitlist only. A fuller notice will be published before accounts, repositories or payments are introduced.
