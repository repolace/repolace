Bars count how many of the 18 bugs passed 0, 1, 2 or 3 of their 3 runs. The outlined bars show what that count would look like if every run were an independent coin flip at the observed pass rate: most bugs would pass once or twice. The filled bars show the real counts: 4, 3, 1 and 10. Bugs are mostly all-fail or all-pass, so the runs of one bug move together. The intraclass correlation is 0.689, which inflates the variance by a factor of 2.38 (1 + 2 x 0.689), so the 54 rows carry about as much evidence as 22.7 independent ones.
